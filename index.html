A fair way to earn

Make ads the side dish, not the main course.

Keep watering reminders and plant records free. Earn first from optional premium tools and a clearly disclosed fee on completed sitter bookings; both directly improve the service.

Our ad promise

Useful, labeled, and easy to ignore.

  • One relevant sponsored recommendation at a time, away from watering controls.
  • Always label paid placements; never disguise ads as care advice.
  • No pop-ups, autoplay, or ads in reminders. Don't sell personal plant or sitter data.
  • Cap repeat exposure and offer an ad-free paid plan if the audience wants it.
